## 1. Cellular palette and imagery

- Tokens in `src/app/globals.css`: ice mint `#E3FAF5` (page and scene field), pale aqua `#C6F0EF`,
  cyan `#69C9DD`, navy `#08283D` (type), nucleus blue `#175998` (links, active states, focus ring),
  soft white `#F8FCFB` (surfaces). The old names (`--snow`, `--mist`, `--tiffany`, …) remain as
  aliases of their light-theme meanings so every module style follows.
- WebGL, not a CSS layer: the canvas clear colour and fog are ice mint (`Scene.tsx`); the baked
  backdrop (`backdropMaterial.ts`, `scripts/make-backdrop.mjs`) is an aqueous field with white
  membranes, four out-of-focus navy nuclei at the edges, white/cyan bokeh and navy ball-and-stick
  silhouettes; the particles are white, cyan, navy and pale aqua motes (same positions, motion and
  pause behaviour); the helix is glassy cyan with navy rungs and a navy reflection floor
  (`Spine.tsx` materials, `Lighting.tsx`; geometry and motion untouched); inactive panels fade toward
  the pale field instead of darkening (`panelMaterial.ts`).
- Seven original cell-cycle covers generated by `scripts/make-covers.mjs`, assigned in gallery order:
  interphase (before division, intact nucleus, duplicated centrosomes) → prophase (condensing
  chromosomes, centrosomes separating) → prometaphase (envelope gone, fibres reaching kinetochores)
  → metaphase (equatorial plate) → anaphase (chromatids to opposite poles, elongated cell) →
  telophase (nuclei re-forming, cleavage furrow) → cytokinesis complete (two daughter cells). Titles,
  order, links and meaning of the seven items are unchanged; each cover carries a small
  "cell cycle motif" label and a descriptive `alt` on the event pages.
- Favicon and share image regenerated in the new palette (`src/app/icon.svg`, `scripts/make-og.mjs`).
  Browser theme colour `#e3faf5`, `color-scheme: light`.

## 2. A reliable way back on every content page

- `src/components/page/PageShell.tsx` (used by `Article`) renders the "Back to Explore" capsule
  (chevron, label, desktop Esc hint) on Events, every event page, Research, About, Get involved,
  Browse all and the not-found page. It is sticky at the top of the reading column.
- Button and Escape return to `/`. The gallery keeps its position because the scene and its store
  never unmount; focus returns to the active title when the gallery initiated the navigation.
- The exposed area outside the column is a dedicated backdrop element. A press must start and end
  on it; clicks on content, text selection, drags out of or into the column, header clicks and
  footer clicks never dismiss. It returns to the previous internal route recorded in
  `src/lib/routeHistory.ts` (home included), falling back to `/` on direct loads, reloads and
  external entry. Browser Back/Forward are detected via `popstate` so the record stays in step and
  no history loops are created. `window.history.length` is not consulted.
- One window-level Escape handler (`src/lib/escapeStack.ts`): an open menu closes first, the page
  dismisses next.

## 3. Top navigation instead of the category panel

- The "What are you looking for?" block and its filter buttons are removed from the home gallery;
  the ring always shows all seven items. Previous/next, the counter, Pause motion and
  "Browse as a list" remain.
- `src/components/chrome/Nav.tsx` + `src/lib/navigation.ts`: Explore, Events (with an accessible
  submenu listing each published event page from the content data plus the Events overview),
  Research, About, Get involved, Browse all. `aria-current="page"` on the exact route,
  `aria-current="true"` on Events for event detail pages; Explore is current only on `/`.
- Menus open on click, touch and keyboard (Enter/Space on the disclosure), move focus to the first
  item, close on Escape (focus returns to the trigger), on pointer outside and on route change.
  Below 900 px a labelled "Menu" button opens a panel with the same destinations and event pages;
  targets are at least 44 px; no horizontal overflow.

## 4. Logo

- The typographic mark (LONGEVITY crossed by K/T/H) sits at the top right, linked to `/` with the
  accessible name "KTH Longevity, home", at 21 px units (previously 15 px, roughly 40 % larger).
  The adjacent name, tagline and introductory paragraph are removed; the home page keeps its
  visually hidden `h1`.

## Limitations

- The reading column and the HUD text are set in navy on light glass; contrast was checked by
  eye and by token arithmetic (navy on ice ≈ 14:1, secondary text ≈ 6.5:1, links ≈ 6.6:1), not
  with an automated audit.
- Firefox and Safari were not exercised; verification used Edge (Chromium).
- The stage labels inside the covers use the browser's monospace fallback when rasterised for
  WebGL, so they can differ slightly from the page's JetBrains Mono.
